One slideshow. Every screen. Anywhere.

What Vinboo is for

Vinboo turns any browser into a screen for your slideshow. You pick the photos or the deck on your own phone or laptop, and everyone else opens a page and types a code — in the next room or another country. No app to install, no account for them, and nothing uploaded anywhere.

🎉

Parties and gatherings

Put the night's photos on every TV in the house at the same time, and let guests follow along on their own phones. One code covers the lot.

💞

Family who aren't there

Send grandparents a code and walk them through the holiday album at your pace — you press next, their screen changes. Much easier than posting a folder and hoping they find it.

📊

Presenting to a group

Open a PowerPoint and share the code. Everyone watches your slides on their own screen, in step with you — in the room, at home, or on the other side of the world.

Your files stay yours. Photos and decks are streamed live from the browser you're presenting from. They are never uploaded to a server or stored anywhere, and the code stops working the moment you stop sharing.
